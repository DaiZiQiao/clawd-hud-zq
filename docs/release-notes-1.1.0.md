# mod-hud 1.1.0

A second mascot, and Clawd drawn closer to the original.

## Usagi (new)
- A new option, `character`, swaps every mascot for Usagi from Chiikawa (fan art). Clawd stays the default.
- Usagi takes the same looks as Clawd and goes through the same scene, plans, motion and pipe; only the drawing differs.
- An agent's role is its hat, worn with its ears through the brim: worker a construction hat, Explore or researcher a fedora, reviewer a mortarboard, debugger a miner's helmet, Plan a top hat, frontend a beret. Any other type and a workflow agent go bare.
- The session wears a small crown tilted on the side of its head; a child wears its role's hat as one cell between its ears.
- Every Usagi is the same pale yellow, with dark eyes, a mouth and pink cheeks on any theme.
- Its thoughts are its shouts and lines: `Ura!`, `Yaha!`, `HUHHH?`, `UNA!`, `Puruya` and more.
- On the puff idle bit it throws its hands up and shouts `Ura!`, `HUHHH?`, `UNA!` in turn instead of smoking; getting up after a fall, it is dazed: `HUHHH?`.
- Its ears stand through its hat; bare, they lower while it sits or sleeps, trail a walk and droop when it fails.

## Mascots
- A cell of the scene can hold two colours, a glyph on a background: the terminal draws it with a background colour, the desktop paints the whole cell under its glyph. Only Usagi's face uses it.
- Clawd keeps the outline of the original in every pose: its eyes are notches in its head and a look only moves, widens, crosses or removes them. No text glyphs are drawn inside a body, so the terminal and the desktop draw the same pixels.
- Clawd's arms go up beside its head, a lean shifts its torso half a cell, its crown is a gold block crown and hats sit over its head; its blanket is a solid quilt that rises with each breath; the child is a small Clawd.
